# TikTok / Reels scripts

15 to 30 seconds, vertical, phone footage is fine. On-screen text carries the message;
most people watch muted. End every video with the site name on screen for two seconds.
Hashtags: #CameraRentalPH #RentACamera #NagaCity #ContentCreatorPH #PhotographyPhilippines

Claims allowed: `outreach/src/facts.ts`. Nothing about insurance, deposits held by
Rentivo, earnings figures or a number of renters.

## 1. "Don't buy it for one weekend" (renter)

| Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|
| Hand scrolling a ₱60,000 camera on a shopping app | "₱60,000 for one shoot?" |
| Same camera on the Rentivo listing page | "or ₱[rate] a day" |
| Phone scanning a QR Ph code | "pay by QR Ph, GCash or Maya" |
| Booking confirmed screen | "dates locked, pickup point shown" |
| Camera in hand outdoors | "rentivo.live" |

Caption: Rent it for the weekend, give it back Monday. Cameras, lenses and phones from verified owners. rentivo.live
